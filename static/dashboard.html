MC Scripts

+
Grand Entrance Script
GRAND ENTRANCE MC SCRIPT
═══════════════════════════════════════

[MUSIC: Build-up track — start 30 sec before]

"Ladies and gentlemen, if I could direct your
attention to the entrance — it is time to welcome
our wedding party!"

[INTRODUCE WEDDING PARTY — one by one or in pairs]

"Please welcome [Names]!"
"Coming in next — [Names]!"
"Put your hands together for [Names]!"

[MUSIC: Shift to couple's entrance song]

"And now — the moment you have all been waiting for.
For the very first time as husband and wife —
please stand on your feet and welcome
[COUPLE'S FULL NAMES]!"

[Hold mic up — let crowd react — cue couple]

[After entrance]
"Please find your seats — dinner will be served
shortly. But first, [NAME] will say a few words..."

──────────────────────────────────────
NOTES:
- Confirm pronunciation before event
- Have backup name card in pocket
- Eye contact with coordinator for cues
First Dance Introduction
FIRST DANCE MC SCRIPT
═══════════════════════════════════════

[After toasts / clear floor cue from coordinator]

"Ladies and gentlemen, if everyone could
please clear the dance floor —
it is time for our newlyweds' first dance."

[Pause — let floor clear]

"[PARTNER 1] and [PARTNER 2], your song is
[SONG TITLE] by [ARTIST].
This one's for you."

[Start song — fade up slowly]

[Optional — invite guests after 1 verse]
"If you'd like to join them on the dance floor,
please come on up."

──────────────────────────────────────
NOTES:
- Confirm if family joins after first verse
- Confirm volume preference
- Watch couple for natural ending cue
Cake Cutting Script
CAKE CUTTING MC SCRIPT
═══════════════════════════════════════

[Cue from coordinator — cake is ready]

"Ladies and gentlemen, we are about to witness
one of the sweetest moments of the evening —
please make your way to the cake table
and grab your phones for photos!"

[Wait for crowd to gather]

"[COUPLE'S NAMES] — the knife is yours."

[Play cake cutting song]

[After cut]
"Let's give them a round of applause!
Cake service will begin shortly."

──────────────────────────────────────
NOTES:
- Coordinate with catering on timing
- Keep mic close for crowd reaction
Last Dance / Send-Off Script
LAST DANCE & SEND-OFF MC SCRIPT
═══════════════════════════════════════

[15 min before end — announcement]

"Ladies and gentlemen, we are approaching
the end of a beautiful evening.
We have time for just a few more songs —
so let's make them count!"

[Final song cue]

"This is the last dance of the night.
[COUPLE'S NAMES] — this one is yours
and everyone who loves you.
Please join them on the floor."

[Play final song]

[Send-off lineup cue]

"If you have sparklers / rose petals —
please line up outside now.
Let's give [COUPLE'S NAMES]
the send-off they deserve!"

──────────────────────────────────────
NOTES:
- Confirm send-off type in advance
- Brief coordinator 10 min before

Sales Scripts

+
Initial Inquiry Response
INITIAL INQUIRY RESPONSE
═══════════════════════════════════════

Hey [NAME] — thank you for reaching out!

Congratulations on your upcoming [EVENT TYPE].
I'd love to learn more about what you're
envisioning for the day.

A few quick questions:
1. What is your event date?
2. What venue are you considering?
3. What vibe are you going for — high energy,
   elegant, something in between?
4. Do you have a budget range in mind?

Once I know a little more, I can walk you through
exactly what I offer and see if we're a great fit.

Looking forward to connecting —
[YOUR NAME]
[PHONE / EMAIL]

──────────────────────────────────────
PRO TIP: Respond within 1 hour.
Speed = trust. Slow replies lose bookings.
Closing the Booking Script
BOOKING CLOSE SCRIPT
═══════════════════════════════════════

[After consultation — ready to close]

"Based on everything you've shared,
I'm confident I can deliver exactly what
you're looking for.

My investment for your [EVENT TYPE] is [PRICE].
That includes [KEY INCLUSIONS].

To lock in your date, all I need is
a [DEPOSIT AMOUNT] deposit and a signed
agreement — I can send both over today.

Are you ready to move forward?"

──────────────────────────────────────
[If they hesitate]

"I completely understand — this is an
important decision. Is there anything
holding you back that I can help clarify?"

[If price is the concern]

"I do have a [LOWER TIER] option at [PRICE]
if you'd like to explore that instead.
Here's what's included..."

──────────────────────────────────────
PRO TIP: Always ask for the booking.
Silence after quoting is not rejection —
it is thinking time. Let them respond first.
Follow-Up Script (No Response)
FOLLOW-UP SCRIPT — NO RESPONSE
═══════════════════════════════════════

[3 days after no reply]

"Hey [NAME] — just checking in.
I know things get busy and I don't want
your date to slip away.

I still have [DATE] open and would love
to be part of your event.

If you have any questions or want to
schedule a quick call, just say the word.

— [YOUR NAME]"

──────────────────────────────────────
[7 days — final follow-up]

"Hey [NAME] — last check-in from me.
I respect your time and don't want to
be a nuisance.

If the timing is right, I'm here.
If not, no hard feelings — I hope you
find exactly what you're looking for.

— [YOUR NAME]"

──────────────────────────────────────
PRO TIP: 3 touches max before you move on.
Quality follow-up > quantity.

Emergency Checklists

+
DJ Emergency Checklist
DJ EMERGENCY CHECKLIST
═══════════════════════════════════════

EQUIPMENT BACKUP
──────────────────────────────────────
□ Backup laptop fully charged + loaded
□ Backup hard drive with all music
□ Spare cables: XLR, RCA, 1/4", USB
□ Backup controller / mixer (or plan B app)
□ Spare power strip / surge protector
□ Headphone adapter
□ Spare batteries (wireless mics)
□ Gaff tape, zip ties, multi-tool

SOFTWARE & MUSIC
──────────────────────────────────────
□ DJ software updated + tested
□ All music files backed up to cloud
□ Offline playlist ready (no internet needed)
□ Client playlist exported and saved
□ Backup streaming service logged in

VENUE ISSUES
──────────────────────────────────────
If power goes out:
  □ Switch to battery-powered backup speaker
  □ Notify venue coordinator immediately
  □ Keep MC announcements going — stay calm

If your main speaker fails:
  □ Switch to backup or monitor speaker
  □ Reduce volume, reposition if needed
  □ Notify client — manage expectations

If laptop crashes:
  □ Switch to backup laptop immediately
  □ Use pre-loaded set on backup device
  □ DO NOT announce the issue to guests

If you're running late:
  □ Call client and venue 60 min out
  □ Have trusted DJ on standby for cover
  □ Venue coordinator to play background
    music from house system

HEALTH EMERGENCY
──────────────────────────────────────
□ Trusted backup DJ's phone number: _______
□ Know venue's first aid location
□ Always have water + snacks on-site

POST-EVENT
──────────────────────────────────────
□ Document what went wrong immediately
□ Follow up with client proactively
□ Repair or replace gear within 48 hrs
□ Update emergency plan with new scenario
